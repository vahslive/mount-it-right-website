Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Mount It Right LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you book a service, we collect your name, phone number, service address, and details about the job (TV size, wall type, add-ons, etc.). We do not collect payment card information through our website — payment happens on-site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and perform your booking, send you SMS updates about that booking (if you opt in), and maintain records for our business operations. We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. SMS Program

Phone numbers collected for SMS updates are used solely to send messages about your own booking — no promotional or marketing messages, and your number is never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. Text STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for support.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited booking information with the technician assigned to your job, and with service providers we use to operate our business (such as our SMS and hosting providers), solely to deliver our services to you.

5. Data Retention

We retain booking records for as long as reasonably necessary for business, tax, and legal purposes.

6. Your Choices

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. To request a copy or deletion of your information, contact us below.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at info@mountitright.com or (602) 555-0110.
